# For Someone Special

> *"A homemade meal, an unfinished love letter, and a feeling that couldn't quite fit into words."*

An animated pixel-art short film made entirely in code — **zero external assets**, **zero dependencies**, runnable offline by opening a single `index.html` file in any modern browser.

Made with love for **Gungun (Bubu)** on her birthday.

## How to Watch

1. Download `index.html` (or clone this repository).
2. Double-click `index.html` to open it in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
3. Click **Play Film** (enables procedural music and cooking sound effects).

## Controls

- **Space** or **Click Play/Pause**: Pause / Resume
- **Timeline Scrubber**: Jump to any scene
- **Mute / Unmute**: Toggle procedural Web Audio soundtrack & SFX
- **Replay**: Watch again from the beginning

## Scenes

1. **Preparing the Dumplings** — Flour, kneading dough, mincing chicken & herbs, pleating, and bamboo-steaming.
2. **Preparing the Sushi** — Slicing fresh fish, seasoning rice, steaming rice cakes, rolling in nori, and plating.
3. **Preparing Crispy Fried Chicken** — Marinating, pressing seasoned flour, sizzling in hot oil, and draining on a rack.
4. **Assembling Her Tiffin** — Packing every dish with care, closing the lid, and realizing a note is missing.
5. **The Handwritten Letter** — Sitting by the window, writing word by word.
6. **The Unexpected Interruption** — A sudden doorbell, an open window, and a gentle gust of wind.
7. **The Letter Reaches Her** — Floating through the air until it rests right in front of you.
